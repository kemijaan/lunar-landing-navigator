# Lunar Landing Site Navigator
### Objectives
* **Interactive site selection and comparison**
    * Allow users to choose notable landing sites (review past misison)
    * Ability to input custom lunar coordinates
    * Consider increase readibiity by allowing users to make comparison between sites selection.
* **Date and time flexibility**
    * Allow users to choose landing dates and times
    * Provide tools to explore how  conditions change across different lunar days/seasons.
    * Make them understand how schedule adjustments affect feasibility.
* **Sun and earth position visualization**
    * Display positions of the Sun and Earth of on the selected time and date.
    * Consider how much time before sunset or Earthset.
    * This directly impact mission operations and power generation windows.
* **Power and communication assessment**
    * Highlight key metrics.(e.g, solar elevation angle, duration of sunlight and earth visibility windows)
    * Help mission planners quickly assess if a site-date combination is viable.
* **Multiple Visualization approaches**
    * May explore creative representations. (e.g, 2D maps and 3D surface visualizations)
    * Help understand the spatial relationships between the landing site, Sun, and Earth.
* **Temporal dynamics**
    * Consider creating animations or timeline views that shows how Sun and Earth positions change over a lunar day or across multiple days.
    * Helping users understand the duration and timing of operational windows.
* **Accessibility across skill levels**
    * This tool could serve
        * mission planners seeking detailed technical information for rapid decision-making
        * educators and general publics curious about why the Moon presents distinct engineering challenges.
